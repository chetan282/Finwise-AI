# Insurance Guide (India)

## Term life insurance
Term insurance is pure protection: it pays your dependents if you die during the policy term, with no maturity payout, which is why it is cheap. A common rule is to hold cover of at least 10-15 times your annual income, so a person earning Rs 18 lakh a year should carry roughly Rs 1.5-2 crore of cover. You only need life insurance if someone depends on your income. Buy early because premiums rise with age. Never mix insurance and investment: avoid endowment, money-back, and ULIP policies, which give poor returns and thin cover. Buy a plain term plan and invest the difference.

## Health insurance
Everyone needs health insurance regardless of dependents, because a single hospitalisation can wipe out savings. A baseline individual cover is at least Rs 5-10 lakh in a metro city, higher for families via a family floater. A super top-up policy is a cheap way to extend cover to Rs 25 lakh or more. Rely on your own policy rather than only an employer policy, because employer cover ends when the job ends.

## Coverage gap
The insurance gap is the shortfall between the cover you should have and the cover you actually hold. Closing a life-cover gap is urgent when you have dependents, a home loan, or young children. Health-cover gaps are urgent for everyone. Premiums for both are deductible under the old tax regime (life under 80C, health under 80D).
